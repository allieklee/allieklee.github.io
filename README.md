# LoreFolk Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 16, 2026  
**Last updated:** October 16, 2026

## 1. Introduction

LoreFolk is a social reading app developed by Allison Lee ("LoreFolk," "we," "us," or "our").

This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, who can see it, and how to delete it.

You must be at least 13 to create an account. The app's store listing is rated 16+.

## 2. Information we collect

**Account.** Email address, first and last name, handle, birthday, and password or sign-in credential. You may also add a bio, favorite genre, profile photo, and phone number.

**Sign-in with Google.** On Android, if you choose Continue with Google, Google sends us an ID token so we can create or open your account. We receive the email address and name associated with that Google account.

**Reading.** Books you save, want to read, are reading, or have finished; page progress; ratings and reviews; notes; and up to five books you mark as favorites.

**People and clubs.** Friend requests and friendships, club membership, club discussions, meeting details you add, blocks, and reports you submit.

**Notifications.** If you allow notifications, we store a device token so we can send a push when someone sends or accepts a friend request. Other activity stays in the in-app inbox.

**Technical data.** Supabase processes the information needed to sign you in, store your data, and keep the service secure. We do not use a separate analytics product, and we do not show ads.

## 3. How we use it

We use this information to run your account, show your reading and clubs, deliver friend-request notifications, keep recommendations closer to your age and taste, block people you block, and respond to reports and support requests.

Birthday is used to enforce the age minimum and, if you are under 18, to add young-adult titles to Discover. We do not show your birthday, email, or phone number on your public profile.

## 4. Who can see it

Signed-in readers can see your name, handle, profile photo, bio, and genre.

Posts you share to Folk are visible to other signed-in readers, except people you have blocked and people who have blocked you.

Your shelf is visible to friends. You can switch it to only you in Settings. Notes are private, visible to friends, or visible to a club, based on the setting you choose for that note.

Club discussions are visible to members of that club. A club's join setting controls who can become a member: invite only, friends, or anyone with the app.

Other readers can copy what you post. We cannot delete copies they already saved.

## 5. Service providers

* **Supabase** stores accounts, the database, and profile photos, and signs you in.
* **Firebase Cloud Messaging**, and on iPhone **Apple Push Notification service**, deliver friend-request and friend-accept notifications.
* **Google** handles Google sign-in on Android.
* **Open Library** receives book and author searches and supplies cover images and book details. A search you type is sent to Open Library.

We do not sell personal information. We do not share it for advertising.

We may disclose information if the law requires it, or if it is necessary to investigate abuse or a security incident. If LoreFolk is transferred to a new owner, account data would transfer with it, and we would give notice when the law requires it.

## 6. How long we keep it

We keep account and reading information while your account is open. When you delete the account, we delete the account and the rows tied to it, including your profile, photo, posts, notes, friendships, and device token.

Reports you filed may remain without your name where we still need them for safety. Copies other people already made are outside this delete. Backups may hold a copy until the backup expires.

## 7. Deleting your account

Settings includes Delete account. That signs you out and deletes the account. This cannot be undone in the app.

Questions: allie.lee0719@gmail.com.

## 8. Security

Access to your shelf, notes, and account details is limited by account permissions on the server. No online service can promise perfect security. Contact us if you think your account was accessed without permission.

## 9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal information, or object to certain uses. Email allie.lee0719@gmail.com. We may need to confirm the request comes from you.

You can also complain to a data-protection authority where you live.

## 10. Children

LoreFolk is not for children under 13. Sign-up requires a birthday, and the app rejects a birthday that makes the person under 13. If we learn we have an account for a child under 13, we will delete it.

Readers who are 13, 14, or 15 may have an account. The store listing is still rated 16+.

## 11. International processing

Supabase, Firebase, Google, and Open Library may process information in the United States and other countries where those providers operate.

## 12. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above. The current version lives on a public page. We will add a link inside the app when that page is up.
