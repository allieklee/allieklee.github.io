Tap **Delete account**, then confirm. This signs you out and cannot be undone in the app. ## If you cannot open the app Email **allie.lee0719@gmail.com** from the address on the account. Include the name “LoreFolk” and ask for the account to be deleted. ## What is deleted The account, profile, profile photo, handle, birthday, phone number, posts, comments, notes, shelf, club membership you own, friendships, and the device token used for notifications. ## What can remain Reports already filed may be kept without your name where they are still needed for safety. Content other readers copied is outside this delete. A backup may hold a copy until that backup expires.
