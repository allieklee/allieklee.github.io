Delete your LoreFolk account

LoreFolk is developed by Allison Lee.

In the app

  1. Open LoreFolk and go to You.
  2. Tap the settings icon.
  3. Scroll to Account.
  4. Tap Delete account, then confirm.

This signs you out and cannot be undone in the app.

If you cannot open the app

Email allie.lee0719@gmail.com from the address on the account. Include the name “LoreFolk” and ask for the account to be deleted.

What is deleted

The account, profile, profile photo, handle, birthday, phone number, posts, comments, notes, shelf, club membership you own, friendships, and the device token used for notifications.

What can remain

Reports already filed may be kept without your name where they are still needed for safety. Content other readers copied is outside this delete. A backup may hold a copy until that backup expires.
